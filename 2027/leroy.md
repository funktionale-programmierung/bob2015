Xavier Leroy is the principal designer of the OCaml programming language
as well as thje CompCert verified compiler of C.  Both systems have won
multiple awards, among them the ACM SIGPLAN Programming Languages
Software Award and the ACM Software Systems Award.  Xavier is a
professor at Collège de France, where he holds the chair of software
sciences, and a member of Académie des sciences.
